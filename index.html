Adverbs of Manner

How did something happen? Learn the difference between adjectives and adverbs.

Adjective vs. Adverb

Adjective describes a noun or person.
He had a quick breakfast.
Anh ấy đã ăn một bữa sáng nhanh.
Adverb describes the manner of an action.
He quickly booked a flight.
Anh ấy nhanh chóng đặt vé máy bay.

How to form adverbs

+ lyquick → quickly · slow → slowly · careful → carefully
-y → -ilyhappy → happily · angry → angrily
Irregulargood → well · fast → fast · hard → hard
Position: Often after the verb or object.
She spoke politely. · He checked his ticket carefully.
